# Warm Ledger

One warm colour against a quiet white field. The orange is never decoration: it marks the single thing that matters on the surface, the way a signature marks the line that counts. Everything else steps back into ink and grey.

Space is generous and left-aligned, like a well-kept household notebook. Blocks sit on a calm grid with soft, rounded corners, never boxed or ruled. Weight does the ordering: one heavy line of ink, one handwritten line of orange beneath it, then small plain words.

The only ornament is the m, drawn huge and allowed to leave the page. It is felt more than read: full orange on white, a ghost at five percent behind content, a pale echo on orange. Its curve carries the rhythm the layout needs, so nothing else has to.

Every placement is meticulously crafted, the product of painstaking attention: margins measured, baselines aligned, nothing touching, nothing crowded. The finish should feel calm and expert, a page someone tended for hours so the reader feels looked after in seconds.
